# Customer discovery interviews

## Recruit
- **Who:** people with the problem, not people who like your idea. Mix current customers, churned customers and people using an alternative (the status quo).
- **How many:** 5–8 per segment per round. Stop when three interviews in a row teach you nothing new.
- **Where:**
  - your customer list (email the churned ones too)
  - Prolific or User Interviews panels with a screener
  - LinkedIn (B2B)
  - guerrilla: café, station, the shop floor
- **Incentive:** cash or a voucher for 30 minutes. Say upfront that you're not selling anything.

## Guide (30 minutes)
1. **Context (5 min).** "Tell me about the last time you [did the job]." Ask for the *last time*, not "usually".
2. **The story (15 min).**
   - "What happened next?"
   - "What did you try?"
   - "What did that cost you?"
   - "Who else was involved?"
3. **Workarounds and spend (5 min).**
   - "What do you use today?"
   - "What do you pay for it?"
   - "What would make you switch?"
4. **Close (5 min).**
   - "Anything I should have asked?"
   - "Who else should I talk to?"

## Non-leading questions
| Leading (don't) | Neutral (do) |
|---|---|
| "Would you use an app that…?" | "How do you handle that today?" |
| "Isn't it annoying when…?" | "What's the hardest part of…?" |
| "How much would you pay for X?" | "What did you spend on this last month?" |
| "Do you like this design?" | "Walk me through what you'd do here." |

## Listen like a counsellor
- **Reflect back:** "So it sounds like the evenings are the problem?"
- **Leave silence.** Count to five before you speak again. People fill silence with the truth.
- **Ask about feelings and stakes:** "How did that feel?" "What happens if it goes wrong?"
- **Don't defend your idea. Don't pitch.** The moment you pitch, the interview is over.

## Synthesis (same day)
1. Write the 3 most surprising things within an hour of each interview.
2. After each round:
   - one sticky per observation
   - cluster by job or problem
   - label each cluster with the strongest verbatim
3. Output: problem statements with 2 verbatims each, plus **how many of N interviewees** said it.
   - Report it as "5 of 8", never as a percentage.
   - Interviews find problems. Surveys size them.
