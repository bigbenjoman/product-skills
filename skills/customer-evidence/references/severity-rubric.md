# Severity rubric (anchored)

| Score | Name | Definition | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3 | Blocker | The customer cannot complete the job, or loses money or trust | Wrong booking date; payment failed; data lost |
| 2 | Workaround | The job gets done, but with extra effort, time or a support contact | Had to call back; re-entered details |
| 1 | Irritation | Annoying, but no impact on the outcome | Ugly layout; slow but works |

When in doubt, score lower and add a note. Inflation makes everything a 3.
