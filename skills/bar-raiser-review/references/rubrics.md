# Rubrics

## Anchors (apply to every criterion)
| Score | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 4 | A senior reader would accept it unchanged. Specific, sourced, decision-ready |
| 3 | Solid. One small gap a reader would ask about |
| 2 | Present but weak: generic, unsourced, or missing a required element |
| 1 | Missing, wrong, or contradicts other parts of the document |

**Pass** = every criterion scores ≥ 3, and no 1s. With several reviewers, the per-criterion **average across reviewers** is what gets tested (a 1 from any reviewer still fails). The overall average does not count: one weak criterion means Revise. Anything else = **Revise**.

Each criterion below gives **4 ✓** (what a 4 looks like) and **2 ✗** (what a 2 looks like).

---

## Business & commercial strategy: mission → goals page
1. **Every commercial goal maps to a product outcome and a metric.** 4 ✓ table with a metric per row · 2 ✗ aspirations, no metric
2. **Stakeholder input evidenced.** 4 ✓ 3+ stakeholders, with quotes · 2 ✗ none
3. **Diagnostic answered.** 4 ✓ product/tech gaps listed, with quick wins · 2 ✗ generic

## Customer evidence: ranked problem list
1. **Sources and sample.** 4 ✓ 2+ sources, with n stated per source · 2 ✗ one source, no n
2. **Codebook.** 4 ✓ definitions plus inclusion rules, 10% spot-checked · 2 ✗ theme names only
3. **Ranking.** 4 ✓ count × anchored severity · 2 ✗ gut order
4. **Verbatims.** 4 ✓ 2 per theme, vivid, redacted · 2 ✗ none, or personal data left in
5. **Interviews kept separate.** 4 ✓ reported as "5 of 8" · 2 ✗ interview mentions counted as votes

## Market & competitor analysis
1. **Decision-criteria matrix.** 4 ✓ the status quo is a column and we lose a row to a named competitor · 2 ✗ we win every row
2. **Feature parity + customer vote.** 4 ✓ forced-choice vote; P1–P4 rules applied consistently; overrides written · 2 ✗ ratings scale, or P-levels contradict the stated rule
3. **Market scorecard.** 4 ✓ normalised, weighted, sensitivity checked · 2 ✗ unweighted opinion
4. **Sources dated.** 4 ✓ every price or feature claim has a URL and date · 2 ✗ unsourced

## Behavioural analytics: metric tree
1. **Definitions.** 4 ✓ retention, window and censoring written down · 2 ✗ undefined "active"
2. **Bias controlled.** 4 ✓ behaviour measured in a fixed early window; outcome measured after it · 2 ✗ lifetime behaviour vs lifetime retention
3. **Links labelled.** 4 ✓ each link evidenced (with data) or marked as a hypothesis · 2 ✗ asserted
4. **Features mapped to metrics.** 4 ✓ every feature has a metric; orphans removed · 2 ✗ orphans kept

## Ideation & proposition
1. **What / Where / How frame filled.** 4 ✓ specific and priced · 2 ✗ slogans
2. **Workshop output.** 4 ✓ HMWs, clustered ideas, votes, owners · 2 ✗ a list with no provenance
3. **100 reasons.** 4 ✓ the top 10 risks, each with a test · 2 ✗ no tests

## Feature research (Kano + MaxDiff)
1. **Decision stated** before any items · 2 ✗ "to learn what customers want"
2. **Items.** 4 ✓ outcomes, same level of abstraction, one idea each · 2 ✗ technology labels or double-barrelled items
3. **Design.** 4 ✓ seen ≥3×, positions randomised, versions balanced · 2 ✗ fixed order
4. **Anchor and Kano.** 4 ✓ anchored; must-be features built to parity · 2 ✗ must-be treated as "best-in-class"
5. **Sample and cost.** 4 ✓ n per segment ≥100, with cost formula · 2 ✗ no costing
6. **Into RICE+.** 4 ✓ MaxDiff feeds Impact only · 2 ✗ Reach and Impact both taken from the survey

## Pricing & packaging
1. **Decision, value metric, option space.** 4 ✓ all three · 2 ✗ "find the price"
2. **Method fit.** 4 ✓ VW for the range, GG or monadic for points; conjoint flagged where needed · 2 ✗ one method for everything
3. **GG analysed correctly.** 4 ✓ cumulative demand from max WTP; monotonic · 2 ✗ yeses divided by the people shown each price
4. **Net economics.** 4 ✓ ex-VAT, store fees, margin, LTV · 2 ✗ gross price × demand
5. **Packaging.** 4 ✓ tiers, fences, free-tier downgrade risk · 2 ✗ one price
6. **Limits stated.** 4 ✓ single market; stated vs revealed preference; validation test planned · 2 ✗ overclaimed

## Prioritise & plan
1. **One impact unit.** 4 ✓ one mode per round; no Reach × £ · 2 ✗ mixed units
2. **Sourced values.** 4 ✓ each of R/I/C/E sourced; confidence ≤ 80% on surveys · 2 ✗ 100% on opinion
3. **Overrides written.** 4 ✓ a sentence each · 2 ✗ silent reorder
4. **Cut line.** 4 ✓ capacity stated; below-line list published · 2 ✗ no line
5. **Staircase.** 4 ✓ base × uplift × ramp × probability, reconciled to target · 2 ✗ a single total

## Approve & communicate
1. **Right lens.** 4 ✓ narrative or deck chosen for the audience, with the reason · 2 ✗ default deck
2. **No surprises.** 4 ✓ pre-reads sent; each stakeholder's loss named · 2 ✗ trade-offs hidden
3. **The ask.** 4 ✓ budget, people, decision and date · 2 ✗ vague support

## Working Backwards (PR/FAQ)
1. **Customer.** 4 ✓ specific enough to screen for in a survey · 2 ✗ generic
2. **Problem.** 4 ✓ one problem, evidenced with counts or verbatims · 2 ✗ several problems, asserted
3. **Solution.** 4 ✓ the experience described; no implementation detail · 2 ✗ tech stack
4. **Data over adjectives.** 4 ✓ numbers sourced, or labelled as assumed · 2 ✗ weasel words
5. **External FAQ.** 4 ✓ price, access, how it differs from today · 2 ✗ missing price
6. **Internal FAQ.** 4 ✓ bottom-up TAM, unit economics, dependencies, feasibility risk, pre-mortem, opportunity cost · 2 ✗ only the easy questions
7. **Decision.** 4 ✓ a reader could say no · 2 ✗ no ask
8. **Into Jira.** 4 ✓ the backlog is split into epics and tickets with acceptance criteria · 2 ✗ no criteria

## Validate before you build
1. **Riskiest assumption named.** 4 ✓ one, with the threshold set beforehand · 2 ✗ "see if people like it"
2. **Method fits.** 4 ✓ usability for "can they"; fake door or smoke test for "will they" · 2 ✗ survey for behaviour
3. **Usability round.** 4 ✓ 5 users, a task script, severity-coded findings, round 2 fixes · 2 ✗ opinions about the design
4. **Demand test.** 4 ✓ honest door page; Wilson CI; decision vs the pre-set threshold · 2 ✗ no threshold, or card capture
5. **Ethics/law.** 4 ✓ disclosure, consent, PECR/GDPR · 2 ✗ ignored

## Build with agents
1. **Spec.** 4 ✓ acceptance criteria a stranger could check · 2 ✗ vague tickets
2. **Verification.** 4 ✓ independent review; done ≠ verified · 2 ✗ the agent marks its own work
3. **Shipping.** 4 ✓ pre-deploy checklist, rollback plan · 2 ✗ "it works on my machine"

## Experimentation
1. **Hypothesis.** 4 ✓ IF / THEN / DUE TO · 2 ✗ "test the new page"
2. **Pre-registration.** 4 ✓ primary metric, guardrails, MDE, n, whole-week duration · 2 ✗ decided afterwards
3. **Feasibility.** 4 ✓ traffic checked; low-traffic alternative chosen if needed · 2 ✗ underpowered test run anyway
4. **Readout.** 4 ✓ SRM checked; CI reported; ship / roll back / iterate · 2 ✗ peeked; p-value only

## Launch & distribution
1. **Readiness checklist.** 4 ✓ product, ops, support, legal, comms; go/no-go owner · 2 ✗ product only
2. **Channel plan.** 4 ✓ platforms or partners with their trade-offs (e.g. dormant subscribers) · 2 ✗ "post on social"
3. **Staged rollout.** 4 ✓ stages, flags, rollback triggers · 2 ✗ big bang
4. **Launch press release.** 4 ✓ newsworthy angle; headline under 12 words; proof; quotable spokesperson; consistent with the PR/FAQ · 2 ✗ an internal PR/FAQ pasted as press

## Post-launch review
1. **Pre-set criteria used.** 4 ✓ success / watch / kill read at 30/60/90 days · 2 ✗ criteria moved after launch
2. **Activation.** 4 ✓ aha moment defined; time to first value measured · 2 ✗ sign-ups only
3. **Churn programme.** 4 ✓ Prevent / Engage / React levers, including dunning · 2 ✗ "improve retention"
4. **Decision.** 4 ✓ iterate / kill / sunset with reasons; deprecation comms if sunsetting · 2 ✗ no decision

## Governance & WBR
1. **Squads.** 4 ✓ by customer journey; each owns input metrics · 2 ✗ by tech component
2. **Rituals sized to the team.** 4 ✓ micro-team: two rituals · 2 ✗ five meetings for three people
3. **WBR callouts.** 4 ✓ normal range defined; hypothesis + owner + action · 2 ✗ numbers without explanation
4. **DACI.** 4 ✓ one approver per decision · 2 ✗ two approvers

## Team leadership
1. **Org design.** 4 ✓ Listen → Deliver evidence; ratios; budget · 2 ✗ an org chart only
2. **Hiring kit.** 4 ✓ questions mapped to values; anchored scorecard; legal points · 2 ✗ unstructured interviews
3. **Agent teammates.** 4 ✓ owner, scope, review loop · 2 ✗ "use AI"

## Recognition & storytelling
1. **Case study.** 4 ✓ Context → Objectives → How → Impact, with numbers · 2 ✗ a narrative with no result
2. **Award entry.** 4 ✓ criteria matched point by point, with evidence · 2 ✗ generic praise

## Decision narrative (six pages max) + PR/FAQ + evidence pack
1. **Reads in 20 minutes:** customer → problem → evidence → solution → price → launch → metrics.
2. **Every number traces to the author's own study or a cited source.**
3. **Trade-offs and kills explicit.**
4. **Evidence quality.** MaxDiff, pricing and validation done correctly (per the Feature research, Pricing and Validate rubrics).
5. **Metrics and governance.** Kill criteria pre-set; WBR plan sized to the team.
6. **A reader could approve or reject it without a meeting.**
