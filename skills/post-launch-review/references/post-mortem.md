# Post-mortem template (blameless)
- **Summary:** what happened, in one paragraph
- **Severity:** S1–S4 · **Duration:** start → detected → mitigated → resolved
- **Customer impact:** who, how many, what they experienced; refunds or credits
- **Timeline:** timestamped events
- **Root cause (five whys):** keep asking why until you reach a process or system cause, not a person
- **What went well / what didn't**
- **Actions:** each with an owner, a date, and how we'll verify it worked
- **Detection:** how could we have caught this sooner? (alert, test, game-day)
