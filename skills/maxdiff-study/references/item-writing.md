# Writing MaxDiff items

## Sources, in order of trust
1. Customer evidence: app-store reviews, NPS verbatims, leaving surveys, support tickets (see the `customer-evidence` skill)
2. The PR/FAQ backlog appendix
3. Competitor benchmark gaps: features competitors have that you don't
4. Internal ideas (label them; they are usually over-represented)

## Rules
| Rule | Bad | Good |
|---|---|---|
| One idea per item | "Fast, reliable streaming" | "Video starts in under 2 seconds" |
| Outcome, not mechanism | "AI personalisation" | "Homework matched to your child's exam board" |
| Customer words | "Multi-entitlement integration" | "See all your subscriptions in one place on your TV" |
| Same altitude | "Downloads" next to "A better app" | Feature next to feature, or benefit next to benefit, never mixed |
| Buildable | "Free forever" | Items you would genuinely ship |
| Under ~12 words | A sentence with clauses | A phrase that scans in a second |

## Pilot
Show the list to 5 target customers. Ask them to read each item back in their own words. Rewrite any item that two people interpret differently.
