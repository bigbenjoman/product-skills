# Questionnaire template (about 6–8 minutes)

1. **Consent and intro** (panel standard)
2. **Screener** (3–5 questions). Who you are, your role in the purchase decision, current behaviour. Screen out early.
3. **Stimulus.** 100–150 words or a 60-second video. Identical for everyone. Include anything you will later use as a marketing lever. If it isn't in the stimulus, the data didn't price it in.
4. **Attention check 1**
5. **MaxDiff tasks.** "Thinking about [product], which of these is MOST important to you, and which is LEAST important?" Show progress ("Screen 3 of 9").
6. **Anchor (dual response).** After each task, or once at the end over the top items: "Would any of these make you more likely to [buy/subscribe]?" (Yes / No)
7. **Kano pair (optional, top 6–8 items).**
   - Functional: "If [product] had X, how would you feel?"
   - Dysfunctional: "If [product] did NOT have X, how would you feel?"
   - Scale: I'd like it / I expect it / I'm neutral / I can tolerate it / I dislike it
8. **Attention check 2**
9. **Profile.** Segment variables (company size, spend today, current tool, age band, region)
10. **Open text.** "Anything we didn't ask that would change your decision?"
