# From results to roadmap

| Result pattern | What it means | Action |
|---|---|---|
| High share, high anchor | Real demand | Build. Lead the press release with it |
| High share, low anchor | Best of a weak list | Don't over-invest. Find better items |
| Low share, high Kano "must-be" | Table stakes | Build to **parity** with the market, not best-in-class: must-be features add no satisfaction above the threshold. Never headline it |
| Low share, technology label | Messaging problem | Re-test as an outcome |
| Segments disagree | Two products, or two tiers | Consider packaging (see `pricing-study`, Good-Better-Best) |
| TURF top-3 reaches 80%+ | A focused MVP is possible | Ship those 3 first |

## Into RICE+ (one factor only, so nothing is double-counted)
- **Impact (from MaxDiff):** band share of preference across the items tested, top fifth = 3, next = 2, middle = 1, next = 0.5, bottom = 0.25. Only band within one study.
- **Reach (NOT from MaxDiff):** customers the feature will touch in 12 months, from usage data or market size.
- **Confidence (from the quality of the evidence):** 80% if n≥200, anchored and the CIs separate; 50% if not anchored or n<100. Never 100% on stated preference. Only observed behaviour (a live test) earns 100%.

When you override the research for cost, contracts or strategy, write the reason down. That record is what makes the next study trusted.
