# Award Entry

**First you have to achieve something.** Recognition follows real results. Examples:
- a product named by Apple or Google among the best apps
- "service of the year" three years running
- a BAFTA for a new kind of service

The entry then has to *prove* the achievement against the judges' criteria. That is a writing craft, and Mark owns it.

## Part 1: Evidence of achievement (Ben)
1. **Pick awards where you can win.** Match their categories to what you actually achieved. Check eligibility windows and entry deadlines. Prefer awards your customers and recruits recognise.
2. **Build the evidence pack before you write:**
   - the result, with numbers (before → after, the period, the source)
   - why it was hard: the context and the constraint
   - what was new: the innovation, in one sentence
   - customer proof: quotes, ratings, reviews
   - third-party proof: press coverage, platform featuring, partner quotes
   - visuals: screenshots, a short video
3. **Map each criterion to evidence.** One row per judging criterion, listing the evidence that proves it. Any criterion with no evidence is a gap. Either fill it or don't enter.
