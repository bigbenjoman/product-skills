# When to hire a pricing consultancy (and when not to)

**An agent plus a panel is enough when:**
- there is one market, English-speaking, and the panel reaches your customer
- the question is simple (launch price, 2–4 tiers, billing terms)
- you will validate with a live price test anyway

**Bring in experts when:**
1. **Multi-country fieldwork**: translation, local panels, scale-use bias, data protection
2. **Hard-to-reach samples**: senior B2B buyers, low-internet populations
3. **Choice-based conjoint and simulators**: attribute and level design, prohibitions, HB estimation, cannibalisation modelling
4. **Defining the option space** requires rights, partner or legal negotiation
5. **Executive alignment**: the answer must survive a board, a regulator or a content partner. A consultancy's brand buys legitimacy.
6. **Weighting** to population, and calibrating stated WTP against observed behaviour at scale

**Scale reality:** a large multi-market programme can field thousands of completes per market across ten or more markets, run multiple stakeholder workshops, and take 5–6 months. Much of the cost gap reflects that scale, not just fees.
