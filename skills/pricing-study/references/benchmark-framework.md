# Competitor packaging and pricing benchmark

**Sample:** 10–30 services. Half should be direct competitors; half adjacent services the same customer already pays for. Include only standalone offers. Record country, currency, date and URL for each.

## Matrix 1: How packages differ
| Service | Content/Scope | Functionality | Advertising | Access (seats/devices/limits) |
|---|---|---|---|---|

## Matrix 2: Commercial terms
| Service | # tiers | Entry price | Top price | Step % between tiers | Add-ons | Annual discount % | Trial (days) / Freemium | Group discounts |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## Charts
- Stacked bar of the number of tiers
- Tier price ladders, showing the % step between tiers
- Annual-discount distribution
- Trial lengths

**Rules:**
- Compare relative price gaps, not absolute prices across currencies.
- Write down what is *normal* and what is *worth testing*. The benchmark exists to design the survey, not to copy competitors.
- Keep a glossary so that everyone means the same thing by "tier", "add-on", "plan" and "pass".
