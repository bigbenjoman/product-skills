# Session guide (guerrilla version, 8 minutes)

**Opening (30s):** "Hi, I'm testing a new tool that chases late invoices. Have you got 5 minutes? I'll buy your coffee. I'm testing the app, not you. There are no wrong answers. Can I record the screen and audio? Nothing with your face."

**Warm-up (30s):** "When did a client last pay you late? What did you do about it?"

**Tasks (5 min).** Read each one aloud and hand over the phone.

| # | Task (goal) | Success = |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | "You don't want clients getting reminders at the weekend. Stop it sending them on Saturdays and Sundays." | Weekend days switched off without help |
| 2 | "This client always pays late. Make the reminders to them firmer." | Tone changed within 60s |
| 3 | "How much will this cost you a month?" | States the price correctly |

After each task: "On a scale of 1 to 7, how easy was that?" (SEQ)

**Close (1 min):** "What was the most confusing bit? If you could change one thing, what would it be?" Thank them and give them the voucher.
