# Team Design and Hiring

## 1. Organisational design (first 90 days as a product leader)
| Stage | Do |
|---|---|
| **Listen** | Meet the product team and the wider business. Review the product development and governance process and the tools. What's working, and what isn't? |
| **Understand** | How much decision-making is data-driven? Where are the bottlenecks and the skill gaps? What are the ratios to engineering? How does the hiring process work? Is there a customer-feedback loop? |
| **Shape** | People plan, hiring plan, budget. New ways of working. Which product and tech roles does the strategy need? |
| **Approve** | HR review, exec review, budget, then finalise the plan |
| **Deliver** | Execute the organisation change. Run feedback loops: is it working? |

## 2. Hiring (structured, bias-resistant)
- A representative panel across Product, Technology and the Business, including one independent "bar raiser" who can veto.
- Competency questions mapped to your values. Every interviewer owns 2–3 of them.
- Every answer probed with **STAR** (Situation, Task, Action, Result). Separate "I" from "we".
- Detailed written notes and a vote submitted *before* seeing anyone else's. Then a debrief.
- Question bank, anchored scorecard, ratios, UK legal points and agent-era roles: `references/hiring-kit.md`.

## 3. Values
Draft 6–8 behaviours, each written as something you could observe in an interview. Example set used in practice:
- customer obsession
- ownership
- bias for action
- insist on the highest standards
- data-driven and outcome-led
- transparency
- team players
- clarity of communication

## 4. The basics
- Introductory 1-2-1s with everyone
- Weekly 1-2-1s with direct reports
- A weekly product leadership meeting
- A monthly or fortnightly all-hands
- OKRs
- Twice-yearly or annual reviews
- Salary benchmarking against market data, done once a year

## 5. Learning and development
- Brown bags
- Hackathons
- A training budget and time for self-learning
- Wellbeing

## Outputs
1. Org design plan
2. Hiring plan and budget
3. Interview kit: questions, scorecard, debrief guide
4. Values page
5. Leadership operating rhythm
