# Hiring kit

## Question bank (STAR: ask for Situation, Task, Action, Result; probe "I" vs "we")
| Value | Question |
|---|---|
| Customer obsession | Give me an example of a time you used customer feedback to drive an improvement. |
| Ownership | Tell me about something significant you took on outside your area. Why? What was the outcome? |
| Bias for action | Tell me about a decision you made against a tight deadline without all the options. How much time did you have? |
| Data-driven | Tell me about a specific metric you used to identify a need for change. |
| Highest standards | Tell me about a complex problem you solved with a simple solution. |
| Invention | What's the most innovative thing you've done, and why was it innovative? |
| Learning | *(Ben's probe, after any answer)* "What did you learn from that?" |

## Anchored scorecard (one per interviewer, written within 15 minutes, before the debrief)
| Score | Meaning | Evidence looks like |
|---|---|---|
| 4 | Strong hire | Specific, first-person, measured result, reflected learning, raised the bar |
| 3 | Hire | Specific and first-person, with a clear result |
| 2 | Lean no | Vague, "we" not "I", or no result |
| 1 | Strong no | Contradictions, blame, or no relevant example |

No "undecided". Submit your vote before you read anyone else's.

## Ratios (starting points, not rules)
| Context | Ratio |
|---|---|
| Classic product org | 1 PM : 6–10 engineers; 1 designer : 1–2 PMs |
| Agent-assisted micro-team | 1 product lead : 1–3 engineers + agents. The constraint is review capacity, not typing |
| QA mix | Shift from manual QA to engineers in test. One real case went from 50% engineers / 28% manual QA to 70% engineers / 6% SDET / 6% QA |

## Legal (UK)
- Equality Act 2010: ask every candidate the same core questions. Don't ask about age, health, family plans or other protected characteristics.
- Make reasonable adjustments if asked, e.g. extra time, or questions in writing.
- Check right to work before the start date (Home Office guidance).
- Keep interview notes factual. Candidates can request them (UK GDPR subject access).

## Agent-era roles
- **Product engineer:** writes specs, reviews agent output, owns quality.
- **Agent operator / platform owner:** maintains the skills, prompts and evaluation suites.
- **AI teammates:**
  - an autonomous scrum master runs stand-ups and chases blockers
  - a coaching agent
  - Each needs an owner, a scope and a review loop, like a junior hire.
